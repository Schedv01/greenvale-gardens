    Common questions

    Frequently asked questions

    How accurate is the instant estimate?

    Our tool uses current UK average material and labour rates for your chosen job type and size, so it's a genuinely useful ballpark. Because every garden has different access, ground conditions and finishes, we always confirm an exact fixed price once we've seen your photos and description.

    Who actually carries out the work?

    GreenVale Gardens matches you with tradespeople from our own personal network — people we've vetted and trust, not an open marketplace of random subcontractors bidding for the cheapest job. We handle your enquiry, pricing and photos; the tradesperson we put you in touch with is who you'll deal with for the work itself.

    Are your tradespeople insured?

    Yes — holding their own public liability insurance is a condition of joining our trusted network. We're happy to confirm cover details for your specific job before any work is booked.

    Do I have to upload photos?

    Photos are optional but strongly recommended — they let us confirm a fixed price without needing to visit first. If you'd rather not upload photos, we'll follow up to arrange a quick site visit instead.

    What happens after I submit my request?

    Your job details and photos are sent straight to our team. We typically reply by email or phone within 24 hours with your confirmed fixed price and, if you're happy, a proposed start date.

    How does garden maintenance pricing work?

    You choose a garden size and how often you'd like visits — fortnightly through the growing season, or monthly over autumn/winter. We'll confirm a fixed price per visit, keep you on a regular schedule, and you can pause or cancel with notice.

    What areas do you cover?

    Enter your postcode in the quote form and we'll confirm straight away whether you're within our usual service area — we also take on nearby jobs by arrangement.

    Does the price include waste removal or a skip?

    No — waste disposal isn't included in your instant estimate. Every job is different, so once we know exactly what needs removing (and how much), we'll quote waste disposal separately as part of confirming your fixed price.
