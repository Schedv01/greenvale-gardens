    Common questions

    Frequently asked questions

    How accurate is the instant estimate?

    Our tool uses current UK average material and labour rates for your chosen job type and size, so it's a genuinely useful ballpark. Because every garden has different access, ground conditions and finishes, we always confirm an exact fixed price once we've seen your photos and description.

    Do I have to upload photos?

    Photos are optional but strongly recommended — they let us confirm a fixed price without needing to visit first. If you'd rather not upload photos, we'll follow up to arrange a quick site visit instead.

    What happens after I submit my request?

    Your job details and photos are sent straight to our team. We typically reply by email or phone within 24 hours with your confirmed fixed price and, if you're happy, a proposed start date.

    How does the fortnightly maintenance subscription work?

    You choose a garden size and visit frequency in the quote tool. We'll confirm a fixed monthly price, visit on a regular schedule (fortnightly through the growing season, or monthly over autumn/winter), and you can pause or cancel with notice.

    What areas do you cover?

    Enter your postcode in the quote form and we'll confirm straight away whether you're within our usual service area — we also take on nearby jobs by arrangement.
